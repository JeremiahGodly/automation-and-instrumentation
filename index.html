How each module page is organised

Every module page has three structured sections:

  • Part 1 – Syllabus theory: Complete conceptual coverage of all topics listed in the KTU syllabus (including topics not yet asked in recent exams, such as lock-in/carrier/isolation amps, pneumatic actuators, WLAN, and DCS control modes).
  • Part 2 – Part A answers: Crisp 3-mark answers with exact points, diagrams, and formulas.
  • Part 3 – Part B answers: Comprehensive 7–14 mark answers with intuitive breakdowns, step-by-step mathematical derivations, ASCII circuit/block diagrams, and exam scoring tips.
💡 Intuitive Learning & Simplified Explanations: Every major question starts with a plain-English explanation of what the device does in the real world and why it works before jumping into math or circuits.
🎯 Exam Success Tips: Green boxes highlight key points that KTU evaluators look for, common student mistakes, and easy-to-draw diagrams that secure full marks.
